Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 25, 2026

eChat ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and disclose information when you use our mobile application ("eChat").

Information We Collect

Account Information: When you create an account, we collect your email address and display name. We use Firebase Authentication to manage accounts securely.

Profile Information: You may optionally provide a profile picture, age, gender, country, English level, interests, and bio. This information is stored in Firebase Realtime Database and is visible to other users you chat with.

Chat Messages: All messages you send and receive are stored on Firebase Realtime Database to provide real-time messaging functionality.

Online Status: We collect and display your online/offline status and last-seen timestamp to other users.

Typing Indicators: We temporarily indicate when you are typing a message to the person you are conversing with.

How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely to operate and improve the eChat messaging service, including:

  • Facilitating real-time text, image, and file messaging
  • Displaying user profiles and online status
  • Notifying you of messages and activity
  • Managing group chats

Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on Firebase (Google Cloud Platform) servers. Firebase provides encryption at rest and in transit. We implement reasonable security measures, but no method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

Third-Party Services

eChat uses the following third-party services:

  • Firebase (Google) — authentication, realtime database, storage. See Firebase Privacy Policy.
  • Expo — app development framework. See Expo Privacy Policy.

Data Deletion

You can delete individual messages you have sent by long-pressing them in the chat. To delete your entire account and associated data, please contact us at the email below.

Children's Privacy

eChat is not intended for users under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
